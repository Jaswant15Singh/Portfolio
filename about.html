I am a dedicated and detail-oriented Web Developer with hands-on experience in Full Stack Development using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js) and modern web technologies such as Redux Toolkit, SpringBoot, and MySQL. I hold a PG-DAC from CDAC-Juhu and a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, both with First Class honors. I enjoy building responsive, user-friendly web applications and have worked on various role-based systems including online complaint portals, healthcare billing systems, and educational platforms. I’m particularly skilled at implementing both frontend interfaces and backend APIs, with a strong focus on code structure, problem-solving, and teamwork. My goal is to continuously grow as a developer and contribute to impactful projects that make a difference.
